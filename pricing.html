Pricing FAQs

Is GST included in the INR prices? ▾
No. Prices shown are exclusive of GST. Indian customers will be charged 18% GST on top of the plan price as per Indian tax regulations. GST invoices are issued automatically.
How does the free trial work? ▾
Your free 14-day trial gives you 1 AI agent, complimentary AI credits, and access to training tools. No credit card required. At the end of the trial, choose a paid plan or the trial expires.
Can I switch between INR and USD billing? ▾
INR billing is available for India-based customers. USD billing is available for international customers. You can select your preferred currency at checkout.
What payment methods do you accept? ▾
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, NetBanking (for INR), and PayPal/Stripe for USD payments. Annual plans can also be paid via bank transfer with a PO.
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime with no penalty — your plan runs until the end of the billing period. Annual plans are non-refundable but can be cancelled to prevent renewal.
Are consulting service prices fixed? ▾
Consulting prices are starting estimates. Final project cost depends on scope, complexity, and timeline. We provide a fixed-price quote after a free 30-minute discovery call.
