Our story

From a bold idea
to a trusted partner

InfoPoint Group Software LLP was founded with a clear-eyed frustration: too many Indian and global businesses were either over-paying for mediocre IT services or going without the technology they needed to compete.

Our founders — veterans of enterprise IT, cloud architecture, and software engineering — decided to change that. We registered InfoPoint Group under the LLP Act in India and set about building a company that would combine the technical depth of an enterprise firm with the agility, transparency, and genuine care of a startup.

Over the years, we've delivered ERP implementations, cloud migrations, custom software, and managed IT support to clients across India, the UK, USA, and the Middle East. And in 2023, we launched our most ambitious product yet: an AI chatbot agent that any business can deploy on their website in minutes — trained on their own knowledge, working 24/7, capturing leads, and answering queries with remarkable accuracy.

Today, InfoPoint Group is two things in one: a trusted IT consulting partner and an AI product company. We're proud of both — and more excited about the future than ever.
