FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can we get started? ▾
For the AI chatbot product, you can be live in under 5 minutes. For consulting projects like ERP or cloud migration, we begin with a free discovery call and scoping within 48 hours.
Do you offer services in India and internationally? ▾
Yes. InfoPoint Group Software LLP is registered in India and serves clients across India, the UK, USA, Middle East, and Southeast Asia. Pricing is available in both INR and USD.
What data can I train the AI chatbot on? ▾
You can upload PDFs, Word docs, CSVs, or paste your website URL for automatic scraping. The agent is trained on your specific knowledge base and answers only from your content.
Is the AI chatbot GDPR and India IT Act compliant? ▾
Yes. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We are GDPR-compliant and follow India's IT Act and DPDP Act data handling guidelines. You retain full ownership of your data.
Can it integrate with our existing CRM / ERP? ▾
Absolutely. Pro and Enterprise plans include API and webhook access to connect with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, SAP, and virtually any other system via REST API.
Do you provide ongoing support after delivery? ▾
Yes. We offer SLA-backed managed support for all consulting projects, and product support is included in all paid plans. Enterprise clients get a dedicated account manager.
Can I try the AI chatbot for free? ▾
Yes — start with a free 14-day trial, no credit card required. You get 1 AI agent, complimentary AI credits, and access to the full knowledge base training tools.
Do you offer white-label / reseller options? ▾
Yes. Enterprise and reseller plans allow full white-labelling — remove InfoPoint branding and sell or deploy the AI chatbot under your own brand to your clients.
