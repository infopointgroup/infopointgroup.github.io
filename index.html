FAQs

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to set up? ▾
Most businesses are up and running in under 5 minutes. Upload your content, customize your agent's appearance, copy one line of code to your website, and you're live. Our onboarding wizard guides you through every step.
What data can I train my agent on? ▾
You can upload PDFs, Word documents, text files, and CSVs, or paste a website URL and we'll scrape the content automatically. Your agent can be trained on an entire knowledge base of millions of characters.
Which AI models do you use? ▾
InfoPoint GroupAI is powered by OpenAI's latest models including GPT-4o and GPT-4o-mini. We continuously update our integrations to offer access to the best-in-class language models available.
Can I use InfoPoint GroupAI on multiple websites? ▾
Yes! The Pro plan supports 3 AI agents (one per website or use-case), and the Enterprise plan supports 10. Each agent can have its own knowledge base, personality, and branding. Embedding on unlimited websites is included.
Does it work with my CRM? ▾
Yes, Pro and Enterprise plans include API and webhook access to connect with HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Pipedrive, and virtually any other CRM or business tool. Lead data flows automatically into your system of record.
Is my data secure? ▾
Absolutely. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We are fully GDPR-compliant, and you retain full ownership of your data at all times. We never use your content to train shared models.
Can visitors switch languages mid-chat? ▾
Yes! InfoPoint GroupAI auto-detects the visitor's language and responds accordingly. Visitors can also switch languages at any time during the conversation. Available on Pro and Enterprise plans.
What kind of support do you offer? ▾
Standard plans include online support. Pro plans add a monthly drop-in call and done-with-you onboarding. Enterprise plans get a dedicated account manager, weekly calls, and done-for-you setup and prompt engineering support.
